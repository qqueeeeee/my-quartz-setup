# Overnight prep for the Prime interview

This connects interview prep back to [[Data Structures]], [[C++ Intro]], and project examples like [[SelfForge]].

This note is about how to think when you have basically one night left before an important interview.

## Notes

When there is basically one night left before an interview, the move is not to panic-learn every topic I skipped.

The better approach is:

- calm down enough to think clearly
- focus on the highest-probability questions
- review patterns I already half-know instead of chasing brand new topics
- get enough sleep that I can actually function in the room

That was the real tension here. I was nervous enough to consider staying up all night, but that usually just makes everything worse.

If I already have weak spots like [[Sliding Window|sliding window]] or certain DSA patterns, the right move that late is usually:

- learn the shape of the pattern
- understand when it applies
- solve or review one or two representative problems
- stop before it turns into low-quality panic studying

This kind of situation is mostly about decision quality under pressure, not just raw study hours.
