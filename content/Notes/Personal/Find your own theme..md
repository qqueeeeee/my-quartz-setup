# find your own theme

i came across this [unixporn setup](https://www.reddit.com/r/unixporn/comments/1rbh1uf/sway_using_gnuplot_as_desktop_ornamentation/)
and it reminded me of something i keep noticing:

the most memorable stuff usually has a real theme behind it.

not "good design" in the generic sense. an actual point of view.

that's what i want with my own work too. not just clean. something that feels
like it came from somewhere specific.

that's a big part of what i'm doing with [[Quartz]] right now. not just making it
usable. making it feel like my thing.
