# sasank kodamarthy

B.Tech Computer Science · AVN Institute · Hyderabad · May 2026

I am a final-year CS student in Hyderabad, and I build software by pulling on threads until the system makes sense. I like projects where the boring parts matter: auth, imports, file formats, background services, small UI decisions, and the bits that only break after someone actually uses the thing.

Day to day, I work across [[FastAPI|FastAPI]] backends, [[React|React]] frontends, [[SQLite|SQLite]]/[[Postgres|Postgres]] storage, and small tools for my own workflow. I am still figuring out where I am strongest, but I keep coming back to developer tooling, full-stack product work, and systems that expose how they work instead of hiding it.

My environment is part of how I think. I use [[Hyprland|Hyprland]], [[Neovim]], [[WSL2|WSL2]], Kitty, Starship, and a lot of config files that probably deserve fewer late-night edits. I think most portfolios look the same, so I did not try to make this one look designed. I wanted it to feel maintained.
