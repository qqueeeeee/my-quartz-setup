# create things you wish existed

one thing i keep coming back to:

if i want to build good stuff, i should probably build things i wish already
existed.

sounds obvious, but it solves a lot at once.

- it makes the work feel more real
- it gives me actual taste instead of random feature ideas
- it makes it easier to care long enough to keep going

that's why [[SelfForge]] and [[Quartz]] matter more to me than some generic
portfolio project. they're both trying to become things i would actually use.

this is tied pretty closely to [[Find your own theme.|Find your own theme]].

if i'm building something i wish existed, it has a point of view by default.
otherwise it's too easy to end up with copy-pasted product design.
