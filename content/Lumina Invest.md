# Lumina Invest

Lumina Invest was a portfolio dashboard i built with a friend who actively
trades stocks.

the goal was simple: make it feel closer to a real market dashboard and less
like another spreadsheet tracker.

## what mattered here

- getting CSV import flows to work properly
- handling awkward broker export formats
- making market data and holdings feel clean instead of messy
- building something that was actually useful to a real person, not just me

the annoying part was the data, obviously. broker exports always look simple
until you actually try to parse them.
