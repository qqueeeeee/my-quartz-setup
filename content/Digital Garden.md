I was randomly scrolling YouTube when I came across this video [Interview with [[Obsidian|Obsidian]] enthusiast 2026](https://youtu.be/NSTT2iBSwZs?si=PmiKESEHER2a3axc), and I found someone's digital garden. A website built entirely from their Obsidian notes. I remember just staring at the [[graph]] view — hundreds of nodes, all with a meaning and a purpose. It was beautiful.

Every single connection in that graph existed because two ideas were actually related. They emerged naturally from the notes themselves, from someone just [[Writing|writing]] down what they knew and linking it to what they already knew. The graph was a byproduct of genuine thinking.

That is the part I care about most. Not the graph as decoration, but the graph as evidence that the writing underneath it is alive.

Now I am trying to build my own version of that.

This site is where that idea turned into something bigger than a normal notebook:

- part digital garden
- part portfolio
- part map of the things I am actively learning
