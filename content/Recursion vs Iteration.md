# Recursion vs Iteration

Anything you can do recursively you can also do iteratively with an explicit stack, and vice versa. [[Recursion]] is often cleaner and more natural for problems that have recursive structure, like [[Trees|trees]] and [[Graphs|graphs]].

Iteration is better when the [[Recursion|recursion]] depth could be huge (stack overflow risk) or when performance is critical (function call overhead).
