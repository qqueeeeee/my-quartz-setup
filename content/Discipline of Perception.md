# discipline of perception

in a crowded cafe, two people sit across from each other.

to most people it's just a normal meeting. but someone paying attention notices
the small stuff: the lean, the jaw tension, the hand gripping the cup a little
too tightly.

from that, a different story appears. maybe there's conflict under the surface.

this isn't magic intuition. it's perception. gathering and interpreting details
other people skip.

people act like observers are born with some special gift. "some people just
pick up on things." idk, i don't fully buy that.

musicians train their ears. athletes train reflexes. attention can be trained
too.

the average person notices way less than they think. psychologists call this
change blindness. it's basically the same idea as [[Vagabond]] and [[Preoccupied with a single leaf]].

what looks like intuition might just be structured practice.

that's the part i care about. not becoming some hyper-observant genius or
whatever. just getting better at seeing what's already there.
