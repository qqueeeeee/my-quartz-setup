# how i use Obsidian

my Obsidian setup is mostly based on [Steph Ango's workflow](https://stephango.com/vault).
mainly the part where the vault stays simple and the structure comes from links,
not folders.

the parts i copied:

- properties/frontmatter
- tags instead of relying too much on folders
- finding notes through quick switching instead of clicking through a file tree

that fits me way better than some giant nested folder system.

## current structure

right now it's something like this:

- most notes live in the root
- `References` is for things outside my own world
- `Clippings` is for stuff other people wrote
- `Templates`, `Daily`, and `Attachments` are just support folders
- `Categories` exists as an overview layer, not as the main source of truth

folders shouldn't have to explain everything. links can do that better.

## why this works for me

i need writing notes to feel light.

if capturing something feels annoying, i just won't do it consistently. so the
system has to be loose enough for quick thoughts, but structured enough that i
can still find things later.

that's also why i lean hard into keyboard use here. i use [[Neovim|vim]] motions,
and that connects pretty directly to my [[Keyboard Centric Workflow]].

## templates

i use templates because otherwise i know i'll get lazy with organization.

Templates help me:

- add basic metadata without thinking about it
- keep project notes and learning notes somewhat consistent
- make links and tags easier to maintain over time

that matters more now because this vault is not just private notes anymore. it's
also the base for [[Quartz]] and the graph around it.

longer version of the rules is in [[Vault Style Guide]].
