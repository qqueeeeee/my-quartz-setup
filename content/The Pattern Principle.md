# the pattern principle

## how you do one thing is how you do everything

I noticed this about myself and now I can't stop finding it in everything I do.

there's a pattern that shows up in almost everything I do. once you see it, you
can't unsee it.

when i pick up something new, game, skill, tool, whatever, I don't really ease
into it. i obsess over it. grind it for hours. configure every little thing. push
It until I'm at least satisfied.

same pattern shows up with:

- with [[Video Games]], where I do not really enjoy staying casual for long
- with tools like [[Neovim]] and [[GlazeWM]], where I tweak everything until it feels like mine
- with projects like [[Quartz]], where I keep pushing the idea way further than the original version

the obsession is total while it lasts. then eventually it ends. i move on to the
next thing and the loop stays the same.

basically:

obsession -> progress -> boredom -> the next thing

I'm trying to spot that loop earlier so i don't keep repeating it forever.

that's why [[Boredom Tolerance]] matters. it's less about motivation and more
about learning how to stay with something long enough to actually finish it.
